# 07 — Factions and Classes

## Faction Design Goal
Factions must feel mechanically different, not just visually different.

## Faction 1 — Slayers / Humans
Theme:
- technology
- discipline
- holy power
- tactical combat

Possible classes:
- Exorcist / Healer
- Gunner
- Blade Hunter
- Engineer
- Templar

Strengths:
- preparation
- ranged tools
- holy damage
- team utility
- technology

Weaknesses:
- less natural sustain
- reliant on gear/tools
- vulnerable if caught unprepared

## Faction 2 — Vampires
Theme:
- blood
- speed
- predation
- night dominance

Possible classes:
- Blood Stalker
- Nosferatu Mage
- Dread Knight
- Bat-form Skirmisher
- Blood Priest

Strengths:
- lifesteal
- mobility
- ambush
- sustain
- curses

Weaknesses:
- vulnerable to holy counters
- weaker in prepared defensive zones
- possibly daylight penalties

## Faction 3 — Occult / Ouster-like
Theme:
- elemental powers
- nature corruption
- arcane control
- area denial

Possible classes:
- Elementalist
- Summoner
- Spirit Blade
- Storm Weaver
- Earth Binder

Strengths:
- AoE
- control
- summons
- zone defense
- ranged pressure

Weaknesses:
- fragile
- setup dependent
- vulnerable when rushed

## First Class Focus
Start with Slayer Exorcist/Healer because that matches the original inspiration:
- damage-focused healer
- holy projectile
- AoE holy rain
- sustain later
- strong PvP identity
